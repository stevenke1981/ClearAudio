a JavaScript port of the LAME MP3 encoder, unmodified, as a separate file.
Source: https://github.com/zhuker/lamejs (npm: lamejs@1.2.1)
SHA-256: 15d285e2587b3bdbfd18a68de6ce07cc074f7480a82c3815da2dc1c348ec6df4
License: GNU Lesser General Public License v3.0; license texts in
extension/vendor/LGPL-3.0.txt and GPL-3.0.txt; LAME notes in
extension/vendor/LAMEJS-LICENSE.txt. You may replace vendor/lame.min.js with
any compatible build. MP3 is a lossy format; recordings are re-encoded.
